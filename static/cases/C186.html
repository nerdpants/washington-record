Sigal Chattah disqualified in Nevada prosecutions

Official / primary record support T2 · Acting officials / DOJ

Named partiesSigal Chattah; Pam Bondi; Ninth Circuit
Financial termsNot quantified · not independently quantified

Documented or attributed account

The Ninth Circuit upheld the disqualification of Chattah as putative acting U.S. attorney in three Nevada cases, finding the post-vacancy first-assistant approach was invalid.

Limits, denials and qualifications

Appeals decision about service/authority, not finding of bribery and not a blanket dismissal of underlying cases.
