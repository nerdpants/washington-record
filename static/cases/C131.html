RESEARCH FILE · C131

Ballard Partners and Bancredito lobbyist fees

Official / primary record support T2 · Clemency / lobbying

Named partiesBallard Partners; Chris Kise & Associates; Bancredito Holding Corporation; White House Office
Financial terms$630,000 (2025 Q4) · federal lobbying report income for banking issues, not verified pardon payment
Indexed sources2

Documented or attributed account

An official federal lobbying registration identifies Ballard Partners as retained by Chris Kise & Associates on behalf of Bancredito Holding Corporation, effective Nov 2025. The official 2025 Q4 lobbying report lists $630,000 in lobbying-related income and White House Office contacts for banking regulatory restrictions and requirements. The filing does not identify the work as pardon lobbying.

Limits, denials and qualifications

Campaign Legal Center summarized this engagement as $600,000; the underlying official Q4 report lists $630,000. Use the primary figure, do not add two amounts.

Records still needed

Compare signed lobbying contracts, invoices, 2026 reports and official meeting records with separate clemency outreach; avoid reading a pardon purpose into the banking disclosure.

Source documents (2)

  1. S83 LDA government registration: Ballard Partners / Chris Kise on behalf of Bancredito Holding Corporation, effective 1 Nov 2025 primary federal lobbying registration
  2. S84 LDA Q4 2025 report: Ballard Partners income $630,000; banking regulatory requirements; White House Office primary federal lobbying disclosure, payer is client
