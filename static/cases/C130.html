RESEARCH FILE · C130

Alina Habba, Concord Partners and Julio Herrera Velutini pardon

Mixed or disputed support T2 · Clemency / financial conflict

Named partiesAlina Habba Mildon; Isabela Herrera; Julio Herrera Velutini; Concord Partners; President Trump; DOJ
Financial terms>$50m reported contractual guarantee over four years · reported private employment contract; NOT a verified payment and NOT a bribe
Indexed sources4

Documented or attributed account

The New York Times reported a four-year general-counsel agreement offered to Habba for $10 million annually plus bonuses, guaranteeing more than $50 million under the contract, while she was a government official/adviser and a Herrera family member was seeking clemency. Florida records confirm Concord Partners LLC was formed 22 Oct 2025 with Isabela Herrera as manager. Habba began work 1 Jan 2026; DOJ records confirm a 15 Jan 2026 pardon and a 20 Jan amended grant. The reported contract is not an established total of cash actually received.

Limits, denials and qualifications

NYT sources suspected connection; Habba denied any role, said DOJ vetted/disclosed her job, DOJ stated she did not speak to anyone about a pardon, White House said she had nothing to do with it. No adjudicated link or finding Habba initiated a pardon scheme.

Records still needed

Obtain the actual contract, payroll/tax disbursement evidence, DOJ outside-employment/ethics documents, grant files, contacts/call logs, and beneficial ownership; seek subject responses.
