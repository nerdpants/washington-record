RESEARCH FILE · C178

Ross Ulbricht — January 21, 2025 full presidential pardon

Official / primary record support T2 · Drug-related federal clemency / oversight

Named partiesRoss William Ulbricht; President Donald Trump; Libertarian and cryptocurrency clemency advocates
Financial terms$183,961,921 · historical 2015 judicial forfeiture amount; NOT verified 2025 private payment or financial gain
Indexed sources5

Documented or attributed account

The signed Justice Department warrant establishes a full and unconditional pardon for Ulbricht following his 2015 Silk Road convictions. During the 2024 campaign, Trump promised Libertarian audiences a commutation, but signed a pardon instead. The underlying court imposed a $183,961,921 forfeiture order; the warrant is not evidence that this amount was received, forgiven in full, or returned in 2025.

Limits, denials and qualifications

Established conviction, pardon and campaign promise are not evidence of bribery or an illicit payment. The financial treatment of any remaining forfeiture requires docket review; do not count the historical forfeiture as a 2025 monetary gift.

Records still needed

Clemency petition/referrals and internal White House pardon recommendations; FEC/LDA records and any paid advocacy; signed federal order and criminal docket; person-specific official response
