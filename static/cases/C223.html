RESEARCH FILE · C223

Phang v. Blanche — September 2026 detailed follow-up order

Official / primary record support 2025–2026 · DOJ / Epstein-file transparency

Named partiesKatie Phang; Todd Blanche in official capacity; DOJ
Financial termsNot quantified · not financial finding
Indexed sources2

Documented or attributed account

On September 16, 2026 the district court accepted several DOJ assertions that specific redactions protected victims and discharged related show-cause items. It required further in-camera documentation for other redactions, rejected DOJ’s assertion that underlying handwritten FBI notes could be withheld as merely duplicative of FD-302s, and ordered review of foreign-language material. The court noted DOJ’s August 27 Federal Register publication and discharged the narrow redaction-log show-cause question while leaving substantive sufficiency open.

Limits, denials and qualifications

The court explicitly declined a contempt finding at that stage. Do not describe the order as proving all redactions improper or finding criminal obstruction. DOJ asserted privacy and review-burden reasons; court treated them separately.

Records still needed

Review current docket and released underlying records; seek subject responses.
