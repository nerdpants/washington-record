RESEARCH FILE · C194

Independent inspection found 85+ defects

Attributed reporting T2 · Public procurement / reflecting pool

Named partiesAtlantic Industrial Coatings; Atlantic Coating Inspection; DOI
Financial termsNot quantified · not independently quantified
Indexed sources4

Documented or attributed account

Washington Post reporting on the contractor-commissioned August 2026 inspection found at least 85 defects attributable to flawed Reflecting Pool liner installation. The inspection separately documented 88 additional locations damaged during Army Corps testing; those must not be attributed to the contractor as independent original defects.

Limits, denials and qualifications

Inspection results concern construction performance, not proof of corrupt contract award or bribery. Atlantic disputed some alleged vandalism-related damage and offered warranty work.

Records still needed

Signed contracts and modifications; agency inspector-general correspondence; recusal and conflict review; complete docket or final agency decision
