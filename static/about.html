ABOUT THE PUBLICATION

Why The Washington Record?

To make consequential public records readable—and searchable.

Our purpose

The Washington Record is a newly prepared independent documentary-news website focused on public accountability, government decisions, financial relationships and primary-source transparency. Its launch collection is Where Business Meets Government, a historical investigative research project.

Readers can follow the narrative or go directly to any of 230 case records. The site preserves the source directory and an explicit record of open questions.

What this site is today

It is a static publication of one research series. It does not automatically gather current headlines, employ an identified newsroom staff, solicit paid subscriptions, or publish unverified reporting in real time. No affiliation with federal agencies or established Washington newspapers is implied.
