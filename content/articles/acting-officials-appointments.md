Power can be exercised by an official whose title includes *acting*, *interim* or *special*. In the Justice Department, the line between lawful appointment and unauthorized exercise of prosecutorial authority became the subject of litigation. The dossier includes court decisions and disputes involving Alina Habba, Lindsey Halligan, Sigal Chattah, John Sarcone and Bilal Essayli. Those rulings concern legal authority to occupy or exercise particular offices. They are not automatic findings that the individuals committed bribery or fraud. Todd Blanche presents a different set of institutional questions. After serving as Trump's personal defense lawyer, he entered the Justice Department, served as acting attorney general and became the Senate-confirmed attorney general in August 2026. The transition makes recusals, handling of investigations and departmental independence subjects for record-based oversight. His former representation is not itself a violation. The relevant inquiry is whether the governing ethics rules were observed in particular matters. [C181](/cases/C181.html) [C182](/cases/C182.html)

The same principle extends to cabinet officials, deputy secretaries, political advisers, regulators and people who hold financial interests through relatives or partnerships. The archive follows potentially relevant financial disclosures, stock transactions, industry affiliations, independent oversight access and ethics-waiver questions. It does not treat a former employer or a spouse's business as proof of misconduct. An honest roster also has to acknowledge its limits. The dossier's officials-and-contractors table is a **targeted coverage list**, not a roster of every person who served in either administration. Financial-disclosure databases can substantially broaden a review, but they do not supply wrongdoing findings merely by listing an appointee. The companion site preserves the currently screened cases and unresolved avenues instead of pretending that the universe of personnel has been fully investigated. [C169](/cases/C169.html)
